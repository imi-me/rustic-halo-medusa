# Recommended opening assortment

Prepared September 22, 2026 from the verified read-only staging catalog audit.
This is a review proposal only. It did not publish, unpublish, edit, or reorder
any product and did not change inventory, Etsy, Shopify, Market Suite, or AntiqueSoft.

**Owner decision: approved all 24 products on September 22, 2026.** This
approval fixes the intended production assortment; it does not authorize
production publication or any inventory/channel write.

## Recommendation

- **24 products** and **102 variants**
- **19 evergreen products** plus **5 fall/Halloween products**
- One fully configured hero hair claw demonstrates size and base-color selection.
- The remaining products cover floral, farmhouse, coastal, faith, western, pet,
  plant, sports, and occupation-gift themes without exposing all 247 products.

The seasonal products can be removed after fall without disrupting the evergreen
assortment. Products outside this proposal remain eligible for later reviewed
batches.
